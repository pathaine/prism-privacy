Privacy Policy

Prism JA-EN Live · Last updated 10 September 2026

Prism JA-EN Live translates spoken Japanese and English on Even Realities G2 smart glasses. It is built so that what you say never leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. We do not operate a server that receives your data, so we hold no information about you and have nothing to disclose, sell or share.

Microphone

The app receives audio from the glasses microphones only while it is open and the microphone is on, which is shown on the glasses and on the companion screen.

Audio is held in memory on the phone for a few seconds at a time, long enough to detect a sentence and transcribe it, and is then discarded. It is never written to storage and never transmitted. Speech recognition and translation both run on the phone itself, using models contained in the app.

Network

The app requests no network permission. Everything it needs, including the speech-recognition and translation models, is contained in the app. It works with no internet connection and is not technically capable of sending your speech, transcripts or translations anywhere.

Conversation history

Transcripts and translations are saved on the phone so you can read them later. They are stored in the app's own database, on that device only.

  • They are never uploaded.
  • You can delete any single conversation, or all of them, from the companion screen.
  • Copying or sharing a conversation is always a deliberate action, and you choose the destination through the system share sheet.
  • Removing the app removes its stored data.

If you share a conversation, that content leaves the device by your choice, and whatever you share it with is governed by its own privacy terms.

Other people in the conversation

The person you are talking with is also recorded in the transcript. Laws and norms around recording conversations differ by country, and Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information may apply to what you keep. Please tell people when you are using the app, and delete conversations you should not keep.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
